Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026 · Last updated: November 6, 2026
In short: Undelix looks for deleted photos, videos and files that are still stored on your device and copies the ones you choose back to your gallery or Downloads folder. Your files, file names and scan results never leave your phone. The app is free and shows ads; our advertising partners collect limited device data to deliver those ads, as described in Section 4.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the Undelix Android app (“Undelix”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information. It applies to the app distributed on Google Play under the package name io.undelix.recovery. By using the app you agree to this Policy.

2. What the app does on your device

Undelix scans your device storage for photos, videos and other files (documents, audio, archives and installation packages) that remain in places you normally can’t see: the system trash, thumbnail caches, app cache folders, hidden folders, the system “lost and found” folder, and media files saved without a file extension. You can preview what was found and recover the files you select.

  • All scanning happens locally. To find files, the app reads file names, sizes, dates, folder structure and the first few bytes of a file (to recognise its type). This processing happens only on your device. We do not upload your files, file names, file contents or scan results.
  • Recovery is a local copy. Recovered files are copied into Pictures/Undelix, Movies/Undelix or Download/Undelix on your device.
  • Nothing is deleted without your confirmation. Files are only removed after you select them and confirm the deletion.
  • No guarantee of recovery. Files that have been fully erased from storage cannot be read without system-level (root) access, which the app does not use.

Through AdMob, ads may also be provided by third-party ad networks and bidders that Google works with. When an ad is requested or shown, Google and these partners may automatically collect information such as:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it (country or city level)
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, time zone, network type, app version
  • Ad interaction data: which ads were shown, whether they were clicked, and related diagnostics

Partners use this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure performance, prevent fraud and, where permitted, show ads tailored to your interests. This collection is governed by each partner’s own privacy policy, for example:

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

We do not receive information from these partners that identifies you personally. We may receive aggregated reports (such as the number of ad impressions and revenue) that do not identify individual users.

Your advertising choices

5. Information we collect ourselves

We do not require an account and do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts or photos through the app. We do not use our own analytics or tracking servers.

The app uses Firebase Remote Config (provided by Google) to receive settings such as whether certain ad formats are turned on. To deliver this configuration, Firebase processes a Firebase installation ID and basic device and app information (such as app version, platform and language). See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

If you contact us by email (for example via “Feedback” in the app), we will receive your email address and the content of your message. We use it only to respond to you and keep it only as long as needed for that purpose.
